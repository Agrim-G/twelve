About BrewHouse

We opened in 2019 in a converted flour mill with one grinder, one oven and a short menu. The room is still small, the counter still faces the window, and most people who come in are neighbours.

We buy green coffee directly from estates in Karnataka and Kerala, roast it weekly in small batches, and change the espresso as the harvest changes. Pastries are laminated and baked in-house before we open.

Kahwa, chai and cardamom are on the menu because they are what we drink at home. If you want something not listed, ask. We like a challenge.
